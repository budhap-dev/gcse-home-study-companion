# Physics

Source: Heckmondwike Grammar School curriculum overviews for Years 9, 10, and 11, fetched 8 September 2026 from the school website. Terms 1 to 6 are the six half terms. Wording of topics follows the school's overview; unit ids in the last section are the app's.

Board: AQA 8463, named on every overview. The school starts GCSE content in Year 9 and does not follow specification order: Energy and Forces come first, Electricity last, and Atomic structure and Space in Year 11. Assessments are "Stepping Stones" (short checks) and "Milestones" (longer tests), with a final exam each summer. The school recommends PhET simulations throughout.

## Year 9

| Term | Topics | Assessed |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wave properties · Light waves · Sound waves | Stepping stones on wave properties, reflection and refraction |
| 2 | Energy transfers | Milestone on wave properties, light and sound |
| 3 | Power and efficiency · Generating power | Stepping stones on energy transfers and generation methods |
| 4 | Describing motion · Resultant forces | Milestone on waves, light and sound, energy transfers and generating power |
| 5 | Forces and motion · Newton's laws of motion | Stepping stones on motion graphs and forces |
| 6 | Stopping distances | Final exam on all Year 9 Physics |

| 1 | Hooke's law · Balancing forces (moments) | Stepping stones on Hooke's law and balancing forces |

| 6 | Building electrical circuits | Final exam on Year 9 and Year 10 Physics |
