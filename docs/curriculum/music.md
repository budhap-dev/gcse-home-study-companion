# Music

Source: Heckmondwike Grammar School curriculum overviews for Years 9, 10, and 11, fetched 8 September 2026 from the school website. Terms 1 to 6 are the six half terms. Wording of topics follows the school's overview; unit ids in the last section are the app's.

Board: not named, but the eight set works studied across Years 10 and 11 (Queen, Killer Queen · Star Wars · Bach, Brandenburg Concerto · Afro Celt Sound System · Esperanza Spalding · Wicked · Beethoven · Purcell) are the Edexcel GCSE Music 1MU0 set works. This resolves the open Music board decision in the PRD, subject to confirming with the school. Composition uses Sibelius and Cubase; performance and composition are coursework, so the app covers the listening and appraising paper only.

## Year 9

| Term | Topics | Assessed |
|---|---|---|
| 1 and 2 | Pop music through the decades · Fusion music including Bhangra and Reggaeton · Solo performance · Pop and fusion composition | End of term listening test |
| 3 and 4 | Classical history · Theme and variations · Classical and theme and variations composition · Solo performance | End of term listening test |
| 5 and 6 | Film composition and developing motifs · Vocal music · Analysis of an exemplar set work · Solo performance, free choice | Listening test on the work so far |

## Year 10

| Term | Topics | Assessed |
|---|---|---|
| 1 and 2 | Killer Queen set work · 70s ensemble performance · Popular music composition in Sibelius · Star Wars set work · Film music composition in Cubase · Solo performance | Listening tests on Killer Queen and Star Wars; ensemble and solo performance; pop and film compositions |
| 3 and 4 | History of instrumental music · Bach Brandenburg set work · Ternary form composition · Afro Celt Sound System set work · African drumming composition · Solo performance | Listening tests on history of music, Bach, Afro Celt |
| 5 and 6 | Esperanza Spalding set work · Ensemble performance · Wicked set work · Own musical theatre production · NEA free composition | Listening tests on Esperanza Spalding and Wicked; final exam in listening and performance |

## Year 11

| Term | Topics | Assessed |

## Mapping to the app

| App unit | School topics that land there | When |
|---|---|---|
| `musical-elements` | Key terms and the elements, theory (chords, keys, instruments, rhythm, texture), dictation | Year 9, revisit throughout |
| `set-works-or-areas-of-study` | One topic per set work in the school's order: Killer Queen, Star Wars, Bach, Afro Celt, Esperanza Spalding, Wicked, Beethoven, Purcell | Year 10 term 1 |
| `listening-and-appraising` | Unfamiliar listening, comparison questions, the extended essay | Year 11 |

Authoring note: if the Edexcel board is confirmed, the set works unit should be renamed to Edexcel's four areas of study (instrumental music 1700 to 1820, vocal music, music for stage and screen, fusions), with two set works in each.
