# Chemistry

Source: Heckmondwike Grammar School curriculum overviews for Years 9, 10, and 11, fetched 8 September 2026 from the school website. Terms 1 to 6 are the six half terms. Wording of topics follows the school's overview; unit ids in the last section are the app's.

Board: AQA 8462. The overview uses AQA unit codes C1 to C10 and tells students to use the AQA GCSE Chemistry specification. GCSE content starts in Year 9. Educake, Seneca, BBC Bitesize, and Free Science Lessons are the recommended practice sources.

## Year 9

| Term | Topics | Assessed |
|---|---|---|
| 1 and 2 | C1 Atomic structure and the periodic table: states of matter, atoms and ions, formulae and balancing equations, the periodic table and its history, groups 1, 7 and 0, transition metals · C8 Chemical analysis: pure substances and formulations, tests for positive and negative ions and gases, instrumental methods | Describing atoms and ions, writing and balancing equations, reactivity of groups, ion and gas tests |
| 3 and 4 | C4 Chemical changes: reactions of acids with bases, strength of acids and bases, reactivity of metals, displacement, oxidation and reduction · C6 Rate and extent of change: measuring rates, collision theory, effect of conditions | Acid equations, collision theory, rates from gradients |
| 5 and 6 | C9 Chemistry of the atmosphere: development of the atmosphere, pollution, greenhouse effect, climate change, pollutants from combustion | Year 9 final examination |

## Mapping to the app

The app's Chemistry units follow the AQA specification order and match the school's unit codes directly: `atomic-structure-and-the-periodic-table` (C1), `bonding-structure-and-properties` (C2), `quantitative-chemistry` (C3), `chemical-changes` (C4), `energy-changes` (C5), `rate-and-extent-of-change` (C6), `organic-chemistry` (C7), `chemical-analysis` (C8), `chemistry-of-the-atmosphere` (C9), `using-resources` (C10).

Authoring note: for a Year 10 student, C2 Bonding is the first topic needed, then electrolysis in C4, then C5 and C7. C1, C4 acids and reactivity, C6, C8, and C9 were taught in Year 9 and are recap material that the Year 10 final re-tests. Chemistry is a Phase 2 subject in the plan.
