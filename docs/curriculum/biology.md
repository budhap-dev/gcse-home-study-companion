# Biology

Source: Heckmondwike Grammar School curriculum overviews for Years 9, 10, and 11, fetched 8 September 2026 from the school website. Terms 1 to 6 are the six half terms. Wording of topics follows the school's overview; unit ids in the last section are the app's.

Board: Edexcel 1BI0. The overview uses Edexcel's unit numbering (Unit 1 Key concepts through Unit 9 Ecosystems and material cycles). GCSE content starts in Year 9 after bridging units. Assessment is by synoptic tests per unit and frequent short informal checks; Educake and BBC Bitesize are the recommended practice sources.

## Mapping to the app

The app's Biology units follow the Edexcel order and match the school's unit numbers: `key-concepts` (1), `cells-and-control` (2), `genetics` (3), `natural-selection-and-genetic-modification` (4), `health-disease-and-medicine` (5), `plant-structures` (6), `animal-coordination-and-homeostasis` (7), `exchange-and-transport` (8), `ecosystems-and-material-cycles` (9).

Authoring note: the school's order is 1, 8, 6, 5, 7, 9, 2, 3, 4. For a Year 10 student, Unit 6 Plant structures is first, then Unit 5. Units 1 and 8 were taught in Year 9 and serve as recap. Biology is a Phase 2 subject in the plan.

Bridging note: the five Year 9 bridging rows are pre-GCSE and carry no Edexcel spec points, so each topic is filed under the unit it leads into, at order 1: `microbes-and-microscopy` (Unit 1), `how-the-body-fights-infection` (Unit 5), `senses-nerves-and-reaction-time` (Unit 2), `dna-chromosomes-and-genes` (Unit 3) and `classification-adaptation-and-evolution` (Unit 4).
