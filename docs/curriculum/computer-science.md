# Computer Science

Source: Heckmondwike Grammar School curriculum overviews for Years 9, 10, and 11, fetched 8 September 2026 from the school website. Terms 1 to 6 are the six half terms. Wording of topics follows the school's overview; unit ids in the last section are the app's.

Board: not named. The topic list (pseudocode, trace tables, bubble and merge sort, Boolean logic, classification of programming languages, SQL, cyber security, social engineering, ethics) matches AQA 8525, which the PRD assumes. The school programs in Visual Basic in Year 9 and runs a "cows and bulls" programming challenge; the AQA exam accepts Python, C#, or VB.NET.

## Year 9

| Term | Topics | Assessed |
|---|---|---|
| 1 and 2 | Problem solving · Algorithms: decomposition, abstraction, flow charts, pseudocode · Searching: linear and binary search | Key algorithm terms; writing, following, and tracing algorithms |
| 3 and 4 | Programming: data types, sequence, selection, iteration, arrays · Cows and bulls challenge | Programming techniques; the cows and bulls task |
| 5 and 6 | Data representation: storage units and binary, binary arithmetic, ASCII and Unicode, images, sound | Final assessment on algorithms, programming, data representation |

## Year 10

| Term | Topics | Assessed |
|---|---|---|
| 1 and 2 | Programming: procedures and functions, arrays, records and files, strings, robust and secure programming | Using and tracing functions and procedures; arrays; revision of Year 9 |
| 3 and 4 | Sorting: bubble and merge sort · Determining the purpose of an algorithm by tracing · Compression · Mini programming project with files, records, and compression | Sorting and searching; programming project: design, build, test |
| 5 and 6 | Boolean logic · Circuits · Application software · System software · Programming challenge | Boolean logic and software; revision of Years 9 and 10 |

## Year 11

| Term | Topics | Assessed |
|---|---|---|
