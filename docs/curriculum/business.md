# Business

Source: Heckmondwike Grammar School curriculum overviews for Years 9, 10, and 11, fetched 8 September 2026 from the school website. Terms 1 to 6 are the six half terms. Wording of topics follows the school's overview; unit ids in the last section are the app's.

Board: Edexcel 1BS0, confirmed by the paper codes 1BS0/01 and 1BS0/02 in the Year 11 overview. GCSE content starts in Year 9 with Theme 1. Assessment builds from 1, 2, 3, and 6 mark questions in Year 9 to 9 and 12 mark case-study questions from Year 10.

## Year 9

| Term | Topics | Assessed |
|---|---|---|
| 1 and 2 | 1.1 Enterprise and entrepreneurship: what is business, dynamic nature of business, risk and reward · 1.2 Spotting a business opportunity: customer needs, primary and secondary market research | Milestone on the first term; 1, 2, 3, and 6 markers |
| 3 and 4 | 1.3 Putting a business idea into practice: aims and objectives, revenue and costs, profit, break even · 1.2 continued: use of market research, segmentation, market research project | 1, 2, 3, and 6 markers |
| 5 and 6 | 1.3 continued: cash flow, importance of cash, solvency, sources of finance · 1.2 continued: reliability and validity · Project work | Synoptic assessment on all Year 9 Business; 1 to 9 markers |

## Year 10

| Term | Topics | Assessed |
|---|---|---|
| 1 and 2 | 1.4 Making the business effective: start-up options, liability, location, marketing mix, business plans · 1.5 External influences: stakeholders, technology, legislation, the economy | Milestone on 1.1 to 1.5: multiple choice and a case study with a 12 marker |
| 3 and 4 | 2.1 Growing the business: methods and types of growth, finance for growth, changing aims, globalisation, international trade, ethics and environment | Milestone on 1.1 to 1.5; 9 and 12 marker practice |
| 5 and 6 | 2.2 Making marketing decisions: product, design mix, product life cycle, price, promotion, place, using the marketing mix | Finals: a full Theme 1 paper with theory and two case studies |

## Year 11

| Term | Topics | Assessed |
|---|---|---|
| 1 and 2 | 2.3 Making operational decisions: operations, suppliers, quality, the sales process · 2.4 Making financial decisions: business calculations, understanding performance | 2.2 marketing case study test; Mock 1 on the Theme 1 paper |
| 3 and 4 | 2.5 Making human resource decisions: organisational structures, recruitment, training, motivation | 2.3 operations case study test; Mock 2 on the Theme 2 paper |
| 5 and 6 | Revision of Theme 1 and Theme 2 | Paper 1 Investigating small business (1BS0/01); Paper 2 Building a business (1BS0/02) |
