# Mathematics

Source: Heckmondwike Grammar School curriculum overviews for Years 9, 10, and 11, fetched 8 September 2026 from the school website. Terms 1 to 6 are the six half terms. Wording of topics follows the school's overview; unit ids in the last section are the app's.

Board: not named in the overview. The PRD assumes Edexcel 1MA1 Higher. Homework is set on DrFrostMaths; the school points at Corbett Maths, Maths Genie, and Desmos for practice.

## Specification content the overview does not list

The overview starts at Year 9 and lists what the school teaches from there. Pearson 1MA1 also assumes the Key Stage 3 groundwork and names statistics, probability and 3D work that the overview leaves out. On 23 September 2026 each of the specification's 97 codes was checked against the pack, and a topic was written for every one taught nowhere. The groundwork is shown first in Year 9 as its own block. The rest sits beside the school topic it belongs with.

| App topic | Specification | Shown in |
|---|---|---|
| Factors, multiples and primes | N4 | Year 9, groundwork |
| Calculating with fractions and negative numbers | N1, N2 | Year 9, groundwork |
| Ratio notation, simplifying and sharing | R3, R4, R5, R6, N11 | Year 9, groundwork |
| Converting units and estimating | N13, N14, R1, G14 | Year 9, groundwork |
| Properties of triangles and quadrilaterals | G4 | Year 9, groundwork |
| Averages and range | S4 (ungrouped data) | Year 9, groundwork |
| Charts and diagrams for data | S2 | Year 9, groundwork |
| Relative frequency and expected outcomes | P2, P3, P5 | Year 9 term 4, beside frequency trees |
| Sampling and populations | S1, S5 | Year 10 term 3, beside scatter graphs (S6, written 23 September 2026 for the same reason) |
| 3D shapes: faces, edges, plans and elevations | G12, G13 | Year 10 term 6, beside surface areas and volumes |

Three codes were already taught but not declared, and now are: R2 (scale drawings), G19 (similar figures) and R15 (instantaneous rate of change, taught with tangents). A2 (substitution) and A8 (coordinates in four quadrants) are used throughout the pack and have no topic of their own.
